Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers to the most common plumbing inquiries.

Do you offer 24/7 emergency service?
Yes, we understand that plumbing emergencies don't happen on a schedule. We have technicians available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including holidays, to handle urgent issues like burst pipes or severe leaks.
How much do you charge for an estimate?
We offer free estimates for most major plumbing projects and installations. For standard service calls and diagnostics, there is a small dispatch fee which is waived if you choose to proceed with the repair.
Are your plumbers licensed and insured?
Absolutely. All of our technicians are fully licensed, bonded, and insured. We also conduct thorough background checks and continuous training to ensure you receive the highest quality and safest service possible.
Do you service both residential and commercial properties?
Yes, we have specialized teams for both residential plumbing needs and commercial plumbing systems. Whether it's a home bathroom remodel or a restaurant grease trap issue, we can handle it.
What should I do if my pipe bursts?
First, immediately locate and turn off your main water shut-off valve to stop the flow of water and minimize damage. Then, turn off the electricity to the affected area if it's safe to do so. Finally, call us right away for emergency service.
