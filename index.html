Company Overview

Setting the Standard in Building Maintenance & Finishing

Sky Links SPC is an Oman-based building maintenance and interior finishing company operating under the reputable Damana Group of Companies (Pakistan) since January 2025.

The company specializes in delivering high-quality construction support services, skilled manpower supply, and complete project execution solutions. Sky Links SPC serves both government and private sector clients across Oman with a strong focus on quality workmanship, safety standards, and timely project delivery.

Backed by experienced technical teams and structured project management systems, Sky Links SPC provides reliable multi-trade services covering civil works, MEP support, and air-conditioning solutions.
